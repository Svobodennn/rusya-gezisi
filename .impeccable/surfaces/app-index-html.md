# Gezi sayfası (app/index.html)

Scope: the installable offline trip page — day view (default), stop detail, taxi address screen, whole-trip garland. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the traveller and 1–2 companions, each on their own phone, opening it several times a day in the dark and cold to read today's three stops, see which is next and what is wrong with it, get there, and tick it off.

Chosen direction: New Year street illuminations, widened on 2026-09-30 at the user's request into a full-bleed Russian winter night (see PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments). Memorable moment: lighting a stop's bulb after the visit — the filament warms and the live wire runs on to the next bulb.

Raises carried from the declined challengers:
- One pitch (Crouwel grid): the bulb spacing is the layout module; type, bulbs and blocks snap to it.
- Live wire (Man-Machine): the wire from the last visited stop to the next is live; connections carry state.
- Unbroken trip row (step row): the whole trip is one garland of 27 × 3 bulbs with a light that always marks today.
- Fixed label grid (box end-labels): every stop shows time, name, Cyrillic name, status and address in the same positions.
- Voids, not rules (cloud quarry): night space separates days and stops; no dividers.

Unresolved: publishing host; accommodation (addresses unknown); replacements for the 10 closed or not-found slots.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each day opens on its city at night — the day's landmark photographed full bleed under street garlands and snow, its name in imperial Cyrillic — and hangs as three bulbs over the dark street: the next stop burns, visited ones glow warm, a closed venue is a dead bulb with its reason beside it. Refuses rounded photo cards on grey and an empty, text-only page.

OWN-WORLD: Winter-night ink ground, tungsten accent, gold ornament, Kremlin brick for Moscow and porcelain cobalt for Petersburg; bulbs on thin wire, photographs as gilt-framed plates with Khokhloma (Moscow) or star (Petersburg) corners, bulb-matrix numerals, Oranienbaum display lettering, Bad Script postcards, condensed sign labels over a Cyrillic-native text face; the Kremlin wall or the Petersburg railing as the hero's edge; no dividers.

STORY: Offline, read today's three stops at once, see what is next and what is wrong, route there or show the Cyrillic address, light the bulb.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cover of the day's landmark with garlands and snow; bulb-matrix date, the city in Cyrillic, weekday in Turkish and Russian, sunrise and sunset; the day's Russian phrase on a postcard; the city's edge ornament. Below: the day's wire of three bulbs with photo plates, and beside it (wide screens, sticky) the offline route map with numbered stops on a line of lights; the trip garland at the end.

FORM: Candidate 4 of 7, New Year street illuminations; seed 5f5207a8.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
